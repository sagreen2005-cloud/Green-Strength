Training notes

Weeks 1–2: practice technique. Weeks 3–12: add repetitions first, then small amounts of resistance once all reps are controlled. Quade should not attempt 1-rep maxes or train to failure. Keep goalkeeper drills short and avoid high-volume jumping when soccer training is demanding.
